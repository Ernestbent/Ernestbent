### About Me
I am a **Software Developer** at **Autozone Professional Limited (APL)** in Kampala, Uganda, working in an automotive parts and distribution business. I specialize in the **Frappe/ERPNext** ecosystem: custom apps, workflows, server and client scripts, and Docker-based deployments. I also build **Flutter/Android** and **Django** applications and manage **Linux** server infrastructure.

I have worked on **EFRIS (URA Uganda)** integration and **WhatsApp Business API** integration, and I use **Oracle SQL**, **PostgreSQL** and **Power BI** for data work.
